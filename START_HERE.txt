FASTEST FARM TEST
- On the start menu choose: 🌻 Sunflower Farm
- Clear the visible weed + stone pairs directly in the field.
- Walk to the shovel rack and press interact to pick up a shovel.
- Each soil bed needs TWO visible shovel strokes. Let each swing finish before the next press.
- Pick up compost and spread it bed by bed.
- Pick up a rake and smooth every loosened bed.
- Pick up a hand trowel and make the visible planting holes.
- Pick up the sunflower seed pouch and place seeds into the holes. Seeds stay visible.
- Cover + pat each pair of seeds; the holes close into small soil mounds.
- Pick up a watering can and water every bed. Dry soil should visibly turn darker/wet.
- Sprouts then appear; irrigation / scarecrow / stem support use the special task panel.
- For final watering BOTH players must pick up watering cans and take opposite glowing sides. No timer.
- The field should bloom before Questions begin.
