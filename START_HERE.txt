TILL RAGE DO US PART — ONLINE v1.3

LOCAL TEST
1. Open this folder in Command Prompt / Terminal.
2. Run: npm start
3. Open http://localhost:3000

ONLINE
- Create Private Room.
- Choose your name, skin tone and outfit.
- Send the room code to your partner.
- Partner chooses her own character look and joins.
- Host starts the episode.

CONTROLS
Desktop: WASD or Arrow Keys = move, E = interact, Her: F = BONK.
Mobile: left joystick = move, ACT = interact, Her: BONK button.

NEW FIRST LEVEL
Arrange Our Home: sofa + coffee table + bookshelf + rug + lamp + plant + side table.
Heavy furniture needs both players. Fragile decor can crack/break from hard drops.

KITCHEN
Either partner may use the fire extinguisher.
